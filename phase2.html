Testing Cadre & Candidate Information
MANDATORY BRIEFING TO TRAINEE (Read Aloud Verbatim Before Starting):

Today, you will complete ten evolutions to demonstrate proficiency in Truck 42 operations. To pass this phase test, you must successfully complete all evolutions and score at least 80% in every evolution.

Each evolution is graded on its own, so a strong score in one evolution does not make up for falling below 80% in another.

Impartiality: Lieutenant [Name] is present to ensure an impartial process. Your Truck Trainer is present to verify training history if required.

⚠️ LIVE EMERGENCY RULE:
Treat every evolution as a live emergency. You are solely responsible for selecting and donning appropriate PPE. We will not prompt you to put on gloves or a helmet.

Safety Stop: There are no real victims in these scenarios. However, if a genuine injury or medical emergency occurs, call STOP immediately. We will cease testing and treat it as a real-world emergency.

Do you have any questions before we begin?

PROCTOR SCORING & ADMINISTRATION REFERENCE:
  • Scoring Rubric: 2 Points (Full Credit: completes the task fully and correctly with no errors), 1 Point (Partial Credit: completes the task partially with minor, correctable errors), 0 Points (No Credit: fails to perform the task, performs it incorrectly, or commits a safety violation).
  • Partial Credit Notes: Whenever you assign 1 Point, entering a note is highly suggested to document the rationale for the debrief.
  • Critical Safety Items (Red Border): Pass / Fail items. If marked Fail to Complete, an automatic failure is recorded for that evolution.
  • 80% Section Threshold: Each evolution independently requires an 80% score to pass.
  • Question evolutions: Select questions at random. The score is calculated on the questions you actually ask.
  • 30-Day Retest Policy: Trainees failing to meet the minimum standard are granted a 30-day retest window. Failure on the second attempt results in removal from the Truck Training Program.

Orders: Truck 42, throw a 24-foot extension ladder to the second-floor wooden window. Perform VEIS (Vent, Enter, Isolate, Search) for a reported victim in that room.

PROCTOR INSTRUCTIONS:

Location: Fire Station #2, Charlie Side
Passing Score: 80% (19 of 24 points)
Time Limit: 20 minutes

  • Victim: You may decide whether a victim prop is in the room or the search is All Clear.
  • Safety: Ensure the trainee is on air before entering the IDLH (window).
Evolution - Aerial Rescue Drill: Aerial Rescue Drill
SCENARIO (To Trainee):

The Incident Commander (IC) reports multiple trapped victims requiring aerial rescue.

  • You must effect a rescue from the 3rd Floor Window (Charlie Side), followed immediately by a rescue from the 4th Floor Window (Bravo Side).
  • You must ascend in the basket while on air (SCBA), then successfully transfer to the piped breathing air system in the basket before commencing the rescue.
  • You will then be told which windows to go to to make the rescue.
PROCTOR INSTRUCTIONS:

Location: Fire Station #2, Charlie Side
Passing Score: 80% (21 of 26 points)
Time Limit: No time limit (focus is on precision and safety)

  • Welfare check: Before beginning this final evolution, ask the trainee if they require a short break or rehab.

Evolution - Rescue Systems: Rescue Systems
SCENARIO (To Trainee):

You are dispatched to a construction site where a worker has fallen into a concrete creek bed with no ramp access. The patient is conscious but reports bilateral leg fractures. No other obvious injuries.

Assignment: The Incident Commander (IC) orders you to use Truck 42 to perform a high-angle extraction. You must rig a system to lower a Stokes basket to the victim, package them, and raise them to safety.

PROCTOR INSTRUCTIONS:

Location: Fire Station #2
Passing Score: 80% (35 of 44 points)
Time Limit: 45 minutes (due to complex rigging)

  • Setup: Place the rescue dummy between the two shipping containers (simulating the creek bed).
  • Safety standard: This evolution requires strict adherence to rope rescue protocols. Any failure to lock a carabiner, dress a knot, or perform a safety check before movement is an automatic failure.
  • The system: The trainee builds a system that uses the aerial ladder as a high directional anchor (a sling and pulley at the tip), with the main and safety lines anchored to the apparatus or a bomb-proof ground anchor.
  • Transition: Pay close attention to the transition from lowering to raising (building the Z-Rig, 3:1 mechanical advantage).
