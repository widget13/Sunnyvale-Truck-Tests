Testing Cadre & Candidate Information
MANDATORY BRIEFING TO TRAINEE (Read Aloud Verbatim Before Starting):

Today, you will be asked a series of questions and tasked with completing six evolutions to demonstrate your understanding of ventilation. To pass this proficiency test, you must answer the questions correctly and pass all six evolutions, scoring at least 80% in every section.

Each section is graded on its own, so a strong score in one section does not make up for falling below 80% in another.

Impartiality: I will be your proctor for today's evaluation. Lieutenant [Name] is present as an overall supervisor to ensure this process is fair and impartial. Your Truck Trainer is also here in case I have any questions about the ventilation training you have received.

⚠️ LIVE EMERGENCY RULE:
At the beginning of each evolution, you will be given a scenario. Treat each evolution as an actual emergency scene. You are responsible for wearing the proper PPE for each scenario. We will not tell you what to wear. For any evolution, you may be told not to wear or use specific equipment at the proctor's discretion.

Safety Stop: There are no real victims during any of these evolutions. If you encounter a simulated downed person, or someone goes down during a scenario, treat it as a true emergency and act accordingly.

Do you have any questions before we begin?

PROCTOR SCORING & ADMINISTRATION REFERENCE:
  • About this test: The Truck Ventilation Proficiency Test is a prerequisite to the Phase 1 test. It is assessed by a Station 5 Truck Trainer together with a Station 2 HazMat Lieutenant and/or Training Coordinator (TC).
  • Scoring Rubric (2-point tasks): 2 Points (Full Credit: completes the task fully and correctly with no errors), 1 Point (Partial Credit: completes the task partially with minor, correctable errors), 0 Points (No Credit: fails to perform the task, omits it entirely, or performs it incorrectly).
  • Partial Credit Notes: Whenever you assign 1 Point, entering a note is highly suggested to document the rationale for the debrief.
  • Critical Safety Items (Red Border): These are the 1-point Pass / Fail tasks (gray on the paper form). If marked Fail to Complete, an automatic failure is recorded for that evolution.
  • 80% Section Threshold: The questions and each evolution independently require an 80% score to pass.
  • Questions: Select questions at random. The score is calculated on the questions you actually ask.
  • 30-Day Retest Policy: A trainee who does not meet the minimum standard may retake the full proficiency test within 30 calendar days. Failure on the retest results in removal from the Truck Training Program.

Evolution - Commercial Walk-Through: Commercial Ventilation Walk-Through
INSTRUCTIONS TO THE TRAINEE (Read Aloud):

For this evolution, you will conduct a step-by-step walk-through of a commercial roof ventilation operation. You will not be using saws or sounding tools on the roof today, but you must be in full PPE and have all necessary tools with you as if you were performing an actual ventilation assignment.

Scenario: You have arrived at a large commercial structure with active fire conditions inside. An interior crew just made entry and reports a significant fire located in the [proctor names the area] area of the building. They are requesting vertical ventilation to help improve interior conditions. The Incident Commander has assigned you to Roof Division.

You are expected to:

  • Verbally walk through the entire operation as if you were executing it live.
  • Discuss your size-up of the roof.
  • Identify hazards, construction features, and tactical considerations.
  • Describe tool placement, ventilation location, cut type, and egress planning.
  • Communicate your plan clearly, demonstrating a working knowledge of commercial roof operations and ventilation strategy.

Be thorough, think tactically, and treat this evolution as if it were a live incident.

PROCTOR INSTRUCTIONS:

Location: Select one: Togo's at Crossman and Java, 884 Hermosa, or 310 North Mary
Passing Score: 80% of 53 points, and no Critical Safety fails
Time Limit: 30 minutes

  • Purpose: Assess the trainee's understanding of commercial roof layout, construction features, and critical ventilation considerations.
  • Location: Do not select a location the trainee has already walked through. Confirm this with the trainee before you begin. If they have been to a listed site, pick another one.
  • PPE: The trainee must wear full PPE, including SCBA (not on air), throughout the walk-through.
  • Realism: The walk-through should simulate a real size-up of a commercial structure. The trainee should be ready to discuss roof construction, ventilation points, and hazards (for example HVAC units, skylights, solar panels, unusual paneling), and to communicate a tactical ventilation plan.
  • Critical Safety items (red border): These are the gray tasks on the paper form. Each is Pass / Fail. If marked Fail to Complete, the evolution is an automatic failure.
  • Lost points for unlisted issues: If the trainee commits a significant safety violation, a critical procedural error, or another substantial omission that is not listed in the task list, document it in the notes box at the bottom with full detail.
