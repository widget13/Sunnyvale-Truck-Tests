READ THIS FIRST: HOW THIS GRADING SHEET WORKS
  • Nothing is saved on a server. Everything you enter stays on this phone, in this browser only. Nobody else can see it, and it is not backed up anywhere.
  • Keep using the same phone and the same browser until the test is finished. A different phone, a different browser, or a private tab will show a blank sheet. Clearing your browser history or website data deletes the test in progress.
  • It autosaves as you go, so you can close the page and come back. Open the same link in the same browser to continue.
  • Open the link in Safari or Chrome, not inside an email or messaging app preview. PDF export will not work in a preview.
  • Grading rules: each section must score at least 80% on its own. A critical safety item marked as failed is an automatic fail. A section left unscored shows the test as incomplete, and a section marked Test Not Given is left out of the score.
  • Timers for the timed evolutions appear at the bottom right. Tap Stop when the evolution is over.
  • When finished: go to Finish and PDF, sign, tap Export PDF, and choose the complete packet or the overview. Then email or save the PDF. The PDF is the only record, so check it has everything before you clear the data.
  • Clear the data for the next candidate. After exporting, the page asks if you want to clear it. Clearing cannot be undone.
