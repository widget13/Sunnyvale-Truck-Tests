Testing Cadre & Candidate Information
MANDATORY BRIEFING TO TRAINEE (Read Aloud Verbatim Before Starting):

Today, you will be asked a series of questions and tasked with completing six evolutions to demonstrate your understanding of auto extrication. To pass this proficiency test, you must answer the questions correctly and pass all six evolutions, scoring at least 80% in every section.

Each section is graded on its own, so a strong score in one section does not make up for falling below 80% in another.

Impartiality: I will be your proctor for today's evaluation. Lieutenant [Name] is present as an overall supervisor to ensure this process is fair and impartial. Your Truck Trainer is also here in case I have any questions about the auto extrication training you have received.

⚠️ LIVE EMERGENCY RULE:
At the beginning of each evolution, you will be given a scenario. Treat each evolution as an actual emergency scene. You are solely responsible for wearing the proper PPE for each scenario. We will not tell you what to wear. For any evolution, you may be told not to wear or use specific equipment at the proctor's discretion.

Safety Stop: There are no real victims during any of these evolutions. If you encounter a simulated downed person, or someone goes down during a scenario, treat it as a true emergency and act accordingly.

Do you have any questions before we begin?

PROCTOR SCORING & ADMINISTRATION REFERENCE:
  • About this test: The Truck Auto Extrication Proficiency Test is a mandatory prerequisite to the Phase 2 test. It is assessed by the Station 2 Truck Trainer together with a Station 2 HazMat Lieutenant or the Training Coordinator (TC).
  • Scoring Rubric (2-point tasks): 2 Points (Full Credit: completes the task fully and correctly with no errors), 1 Point (Partial Credit: completes the task partially with minor, correctable errors), 0 Points (No Credit: fails to perform the task, omits it entirely, or performs it incorrectly).
  • Partial Credit Notes: Whenever you assign 1 Point, entering a note is highly suggested to document the rationale for the debrief.
  • Critical Safety Items (Red Border): These are the 1-point Pass / Fail tasks (gray on the paper form). If marked Fail to Complete, an automatic failure is recorded for that evolution.
  • 80% Section Threshold: The questions and each evolution independently require an 80% score to pass.
  • Questions: Select questions at random. The score is calculated on the questions you actually ask.
  • 30-Day Retest Policy: A trainee who does not meet the minimum standard gets a single retest, which must be completed within 30 calendar days. Failure on the retest results in removal from the Truck Training Program.
