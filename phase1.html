Testing Cadre & Candidate Information
MANDATORY BRIEFING TO TRAINEE (Read Aloud Verbatim Before Starting):

Today, you will complete the Phase 1 Truck 45 evaluation covering eight evolutions: Apparatus Specs, Driving, Stabilizers, Ladder Operations, Window Rescues, Pumping Operations, Forcible Entry, and Rope Rescue. To pass this qualification evaluation, you must successfully complete all administered scenarios and score at least 80% in every section.

Each section is graded on its own, so a strong score in one section does not make up for falling below 80% in another.

I am your Proctor for this evaluation. Lieutenant [Name] is present as the Supervisor to ensure an impartial and standardized process, and your Truck Trainer is onsite to verify training records as needed.

⚠️ LIVE EMERGENCY & PPE PRE-PLANNING RULE:
Treat every scenario-based evolution as an actual live emergency. You must pre-plan your operations and determine your own Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) appropriate for the hazards presented. You will NOT be prompted or instructed by the cadre on what to wear or stage. If you need clarification on simulated fire, building, or smoke conditions, you are expected to ask the Proctor directly.

Safety Briefing: There are no actual victims in these scenarios. However, if a genuine injury or medical incident occurs involving you, the staff, or a bystander, stop the evolution immediately and treat it as a real-world emergency.

Do you have any questions before we begin?

PROCTOR SCORING & ADMINISTRATION REFERENCE:
  • Scoring Rubric: 2 Points (Full Credit / Completed), 1 Point (Partial Credit - deduction note is highly suggested, but not mandatory), 0 Points (Fail / Failed to Complete).
  • Partial Credit Notes: Whenever you assign 1 Point, entering a note is highly suggested to document the specific rationale for candidate debriefing.
  • Critical Safety Items (Red Border): Mandatory safety evolutions (e.g., Four B's check, Stabilizer shake/pins, tip lock-in, door control, pulley placement). If marked Fail to Complete, an immediate automatic failure is recorded for that evolution.
  • 80% Section Threshold: Each active testing section independently requires an 80% score to pass.
  • 30-Day Retest Policy: Trainees failing to meet the minimum standard are granted a 30-day retest window. Failure on the second attempt results in removal from the Truck Training Program.
